## 🏨 Honest Review -- Belpahari Holiday Home

We stayed at a Holiday Home in Belpahari. Here's our honest feedback:

❌ Poor Management\
❌ High Food Prices compared to quality\
⚠️ Service needs improvement

If you're planning to stay there, manage expectations and confirm
pricing beforehand.

## 🏰 Important Tip -- Jhargram Rajbari Visit

If you're planning to visit Jhargram Rajbari, make sure to pre-book your
entry tickets online. Without prior booking, entry may not be allowed.

This ride was full of adventure, brotherhood, forest vibes, and pure
riding spirit! 💪🔥
