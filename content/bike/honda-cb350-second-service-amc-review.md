# Honda CB350 2nd Service Complete – Is the ₹8,592 AMC Worth It?

After completing the second free service of my Honda CB350 at Honda BigWing, I decided to share the complete service experience, maintenance work performed, actual costs involved, and whether the 2-Year Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) priced at ₹8,592 makes financial sense for long-term ownership.

If you're a Honda CB350, H'ness CB350, or CB350RS owner, this breakdown should help you understand the expected maintenance expenses and evaluate whether the AMC is worth purchasing.

## Service Work Performed

During the second service visit, the following maintenance tasks were completed:

* Engine oil replacement
* Oil filter replacement
* Air filter inspection
* Chain cleaning and lubrication
* Vehicle washing and polishing
* Brake fluid inspection
* General vehicle health check

## Honda CB350 AMC Details

Honda BigWing offers a 2-Year Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) that covers several routine service items.

### AMC Coverage

* 4 Engine Oil Replacements
* 4 Washers
* 1 Brake Fluid Change
* 1 Oil Filter
* 1 O-Ring
* 2 Spark Plugs
* 1 Air Filter
* 5% Discount on Parts & Lubricants
* 20% Discount on Labour Charges
* Washing & Polishing
* Chain Lubrication
* Vehicle Health Check

### AMC Price

₹8,592 for 2 Years

## Is the Honda AMC Worth Buying?

The answer depends on how frequently you ride and how long you plan to keep the motorcycle.

### Advantages

* Predictable maintenance expenses
* Savings on labour charges
* Included consumables reduce service costs
* Peace of mind for regular riders

### Things to Consider

* Riders with low annual mileage may not fully utilize all benefits.
* Compare the AMC price with your expected service expenses over two years.
* Check whether you intend to continue servicing exclusively at Honda BigWing.
