# 🌄 About Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath Temple is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and an important part of the Char Dham Yatra.

Located at an altitude of approximately 3,583 meters in the Himalayas, Kedarnath is among the most revered pilgrimage destinations in India.

Every year thousands of devotees undertake this spiritual journey to seek the blessings of Lord Shiva.
