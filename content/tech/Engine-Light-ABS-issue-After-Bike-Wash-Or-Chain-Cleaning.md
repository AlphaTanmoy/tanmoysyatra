# Honda CB350 Engine Check Light & ABS Light ON After Wash? Here's What Happened!

Watch the full video on YouTube: [https://www.youtube.com/shorts/NGVd-d6gO4M](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/NGVd-d6gO4M)

After washing my Honda CB350 at home using a pressure washer, I suddenly faced a scary situation — both the Engine Check Light and ABS Light turned ON.

In this video, I explain exactly what went wrong, what mistakes to avoid while bike washing, and how the issue was solved without any major repair.

## What Happened

- Bike was washed using a pressure washer (spray mode)
- Chain cleaned with Motul Chain Cleaner & lubed properly
- Silicone spray applied near key slots and engine area
- After the wash, the Engine Check Light and ABS Light stayed ON

## How We Fixed It

- Allowed the bike to dry completely
- Checked the ABS sensor and wiring for moisture
- Rode the bike gently for a few kilometers

The warning lights turned off automatically once the moisture cleared.

## Lesson Learned

**Avoid spraying high-pressure water near:**
- Sensors
- Wiring harnesses
- ECU area
- ABS ring and sensor
- Electrical components
